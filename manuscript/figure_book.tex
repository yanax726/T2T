These describe the nested fitting procedure, not an independent test of the final all-development coefficient vector. W0/W12 used patient-grouped model selection; W24 is previously inspected same-cohort temporal application. EASI >21 counts show the marked change in severity mix. Full numerical metrics, severity strata, calibration, area-only controls and assay sensitivity analyses are supplied in source tables.
