\section{Discussion}
A common four-input model-development procedure connected nominal-visit-matched affected skin area and serum measurements to EASI throughout this Chinese treatment cohort. Its strongest performance was in follow-up disease activity, where most estimates were within two EASI points. Using the same calculation across visits also allowed changes to be expressed directly in EASI units. This makes the formula straightforward to examine and reproduce: the research equation takes BSA and three serum concentrations without a treatment label or previous score. Same-day clinical/serum matching was not verified, so its use as a contemporaneous clinical measurement requires a defined sampling window.

The equation combines two aspects of disease that have different clinical roles. BSA represents the extent of visible lesions, an established component of EASI.\citep{hanifin2022,home_easi} The molecular term adjusts that extent-based estimate using serum measurements. The full 23-marker analysis places those inputs within treatment-dependent inflammatory changes, rather than treating them as an exhaustive map of disease biology. Coefficient signs describe conditional prediction in this dataset; the negative IL-5 term, for example, is not evidence of a protective effect. Molecular added accuracy was not established in the matched controls, so the biological findings do not establish a prediction benefit from ordering these assays. A future demonstration of molecular value would need independent evidence, such as explaining clinically adjudicated blood--skin discordance or anticipating a later outcome beyond the current EASI score.

Individual monitoring is a distinct test from agreement at a single visit. The formula reproduced much of the ordering of clinical improvement, yet changes beginning at baseline were less precise than later follow-up changes. This pattern locates the main unresolved problem in untreated, often severe disease, rather than in choosing a separate formula for each treatment. Lesion intensity can vary despite similar affected area. Chinese serum endotypes and recent severity-linked proteomic findings also show broader inflammatory and epithelial-associated heterogeneity than a three-marker panel can represent.\citep{wu2023,lally2026} These observations motivate testing additional lesion information in severe disease, but they do not identify the cause of individual errors or establish that more blood markers will reduce them.

The p-EASI studies provide the closest precedent for a usable serum-to-EASI calculation.\citep{thijs2017,thijs2019,bakker2020} Their correlations and classification results encouraged molecular estimation; the present study adds explicit absolute errors, their distribution and within-person response checks in a local treatment cohort. Their use of serum alone differs from the present BSA--serum inputs. Measurement procedures can affect calibration, and both assay compatibility and case mix matter when moving a prediction rule between settings.\citep{luijken2020,riley2024validation} The source-table local-application analyses therefore provide neither a fair superiority test nor evidence that population ethnicity explains a performance difference.

Treatment context matters both for interpreting biomarkers and for comparing clinical outcomes. Patients receiving the higher initial abrocitinib dose had greater starting severity; subsequent reductions were selected after clinical response. Baseline adjustment and explicit maintenance pathways therefore address different questions. This distinction is consistent with randomized abrocitinib induction/withdrawal designs and observational dupilumab tapering studies, which define eligibility for maintenance or reduction before evaluating its success.\citep{blauvelt2022regimen,spekhorst2023} The present results describe adjusted initial-strategy associations and observed later management, rather than the effect of assigning each patient an alternative dose. Differential missingness, endpoint-specific residual imbalance and response-selected follow-up limit these comparisons even after baseline adjustment. They also preserve itch and quality-of-life outcomes as complementary clinical domains; neither is adequately recovered by the same small serum--BSA panel.

Independent evaluation should now test the frozen equation, the assay procedure and the intended clinical decision together. Patient-level validation prevented a person's repeated visits from appearing in both training and testing, but the molecular panel had been selected earlier and week 24 had already been inspected in prior project analyses. The current evidence consequently supports within-cohort performance of the development procedure, with a clearly defined research formula for the next evaluation. Patient bootstrap intervals condition on saved predictions and omit model-development uncertainty; no independent validation of the single final formula has been performed. Follow-up overestimation and calibration slopes below one also argue against substituting estimates for clinical examination. Adequate numbers of active severe cases, independent clinical assessment and a prespecified tolerance for individual errors will determine whether the same simple calculation can support decisions across the full disease range.
