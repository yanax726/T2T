LDA requires EASI $\leq$7 AND PP-NRS $\leq$4; vLDA requires EASI $\leq$3 AND PP-NRS $\leq$1. Percentages and Wilson 95\% confidence intervals use patients with both observed scores at the same nominal visit. Joint n is that observed denominator; full N is the initial treatment population. Full-group bounds assign every unresolved AND state to failure or success, while any observed failing component remains a failure. These bounds are missing-outcome scenarios, not confidence intervals. They can differ from the observed-joint rate because a partially measured patient can already be known to fail. The analysis is post hoc, with no imputed clinical scores or new between-treatment significance tests. It evaluates a single-visit state, not individualized AHEAD MDA, six-month continuous control, remission or a causal treatment effect.
