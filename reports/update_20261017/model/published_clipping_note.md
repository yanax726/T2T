# Matched output-bound sensitivity

The published rounded p-EASI formula was additionally restricted to 0–72, matching the local estimator's output bounds. One of 60 baseline predictions was below zero; none exceeded 72. Clipping changed the published baseline MAE from 9.163 to 9.123 and CCC from 0.403 to 0.406, with unchanged Spearman correlation and severity-band accuracy. All 12 week-12 and 13 week-24 published predictions were already within 0–72, so their MAE, correlation and agreement were unchanged. The comparison remains conditional on the small complete-assay subset and the unbridged IL2RA/sIL-2R assay correspondence. Primary raw published results were retained.
