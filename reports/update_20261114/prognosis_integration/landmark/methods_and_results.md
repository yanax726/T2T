# Week-12 serum levels and week-24 EASI75

The analysis protocol was saved before this fit. It uses the current age-at-least-12, unambiguous-initial-treatment population. Eligibility requires measured W0 EASI>0, a W12 clinical record and measured W24 EASI. W24 EASI75 means W24 EASI <=25% of the measured W0 EASI; it is not an additional 75% improvement from W12.

Of 93 clinically evaluable patients, 68 had all three W12 measurements (59 W24 responders, 9 nonresponders), and 72 had at least one marker (63/9). The fixed markers were CCL17, CCL26 and IL-5. The sensitivity replaces only missing predictors using each training fold, never the W24 response. The clinical comparator uses age, sex, BMI, centre, initial treatment, W0 EASI and W12 EASI, PP-NRS, recorded IGA, BSA and adult DLQI. Under-18 DLQI is masked. No W24 information enters a predictor; initial treatment is not a causal representation of later dose changes.

Ridge logistic models, separate for serum only, clinical only and combined inputs, share five stratified patient folds repeated five times. Three-fold inner validation selects C=0.01/0.1/1 by log-loss. Imputation, scaling and one-hot encoding are inside the training pipeline. The primary evaluation averages metrics scored within each repetition. A second aggregation scores patient probabilities averaged across repetitions, labelled separately. Conditional intervals use 2,000 paired patient bootstrap draws of saved predictions without refitting or repeating historical model selection.

Primary mean-repetition AUROC was 0.406 (95% CI 0.267–0.544) for serum only, 0.817 (0.681–0.941) for clinical inputs and 0.789 (0.623–0.940) for their combination. Combined-minus-clinical AUROC was −0.0286 (−0.0828 to 0.0124), and Brier difference +0.00096 (−0.00355 to 0.00524). Brier scores were 0.1160, 0.0930 and 0.0940, respectively; training-fold prevalence predictions gave 0.1152. Serum-only AUPRC 0.837 should be read against the observed 0.868 event prevalence. High overall responder frequency does not demonstrate useful serum discrimination.

The 72-patient predictor-imputation sensitivity gave serum/clinical/combined AUROCs 0.372/0.793/0.783. Combined-minus-clinical AUROC was −0.0106 (−0.0486 to 0.0325), and Brier difference +0.00588 (−0.00095 to 0.01455). No fixed-three-marker forecast or incremental benefit was established. An AUROC below 0.5 must not be flipped after seeing held-out outcomes to manufacture a favorable predictor.

These are internally validated exploratory procedures with only nine nonresponders, not an external test or a deployable final prognostic equation. They differ from both contemporaneous E-EASI and the older W12 landmark analysis that used W0-to-W12 changes in a larger molecular panel. All candidates, both populations and both evaluation aggregations are retained.

The fit used 150 outer models and 450 stored inner partitions, with no patient crossing. All selected fits terminated in at most 16 iterations, below the 3,000 cap. The independent verifier reproduces patient eligibility, endpoint labels, exact seeded outer/inner partitions, point metrics, pairwise-rank AUROC and count-weighted patient-bootstrap AUROC/Brier intervals and differences. Protected sources, frozen E-EASI parameters and old results are unchanged. Individual predictions and memberships remain restricted.
